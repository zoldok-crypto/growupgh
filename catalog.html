Three ranges. Four decisions.

Every greenhouse in the catalogue is defined by the same four choices — pick the range first, then tune the frame to your climate and the glazing to your crops.

  • A Arch step 1 m — standard frame for sheltered gardens. 0,67 m — reinforced frame for mountain regions, heavy snow and strong wind.
  • B Polycarbonate 4 mm — light and bright, the everyday choice. 6 mm — warmer and tougher against hail.
  • C Profile 40×20 — standard steel profile. 25×20 Light — lighter GAUDI SL frames for smaller gardens.
  • D Length Metal greenhouses from 2 to 14 m. Extend any time with a 2 m extension module.
